Constitution Lab • Explore Constitutional Design

How should power work? Compare. Test. Design.

A hands-on studio for constitutional design. Compare how democracies answered the same questions — who runs the government, who checks them, how votes become power — test the trade-offs with real crises, and combine choices into your own architecture.

▶ Start the adventure — Can you survive the week? Explore: Who's in charge?

How it works

1. Live it — Step into a scenario. You have a role, a crisis, and three choices.

2. Rewind it — Play the same crisis with different rules. Feel why institutions matter, not just how they sound.

3. Compare it — Same question, five answers side-by-side — with evidence from real constitutions.

No lectures. No “correct” design. Just trade-offs made visible.
