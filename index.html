A neutral, modular, evidence-based studio for constitutional design. Compare institutions, test trade-offs with real cases, combine choices into your own architecture.

Two complementary projects

The Federated Republic — one complete constitutional system, demonstrated through Torenthia. A finished building you can walk through.

The Constitution Lab — the design studio. No house style. Pick a question (who runs the government? how to protect rights?) and compare how democracies answered it.

The FR becomes one reference model among others in the Lab — not the answer you're expected to arrive at.

Neutrality

No preferred answer

Every option gets the same template, same evidence length, same card size. The Lab has no house system. FR appears once per module as “one implementation among others.”

Modularity

Explore one piece

You don't need to adopt a whole constitution. Dive into Head of Government, Legislature, or Rights — 7 minutes each. Combine later if you want.

Evidence

Real cases, not just hypotheticals

Each mechanism links to 3–5 real constitutions and what happened: Germany’s constructive vote, France’s cohabitation, Brazil’s deadlock.

Explorer modules

Each module: Meet the options → Compare → Break it → Your Architecture. Deterministic rules, optional AI for weird combos.
